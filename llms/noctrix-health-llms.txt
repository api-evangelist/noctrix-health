## Products and Solutions

### Nidra® Therapy (NTX150 TOMAC System)
- **What it is**: A wearable medical device that uses Tonic Motor Activation (TOMAC) to stimulate nerves in the legs, engaging the same neural pathways as voluntary movement
- **Indication**: The Nidra Tonic Motor Activation® (TOMAC®) System is intended to reduce symptoms of primary moderate-severe Restless Legs Syndrome and to improve sleep quality in adults refractory to medications. **Rx Only.**
- **Clinical results** (as stated on nidrarls.com):
  - 91% of patients reported an improvement in RLS symptoms¹
  - 11.3-point reduction in IRLS score at 32 weeks²
  - 2.72 fewer days with RLS symptoms per week at 32 weeks²
  - Symptom relief in less than 30 minutes³
- **Contraindications**: epilepsy or other seizure disorder; active medical device implant anywhere in the body (pacemakers, spinal cord stimulators, deep brain stimulators); metal implant in the leg at the therapy site (not including knee replacements); known allergy to device materials or severe previous reaction to medical adhesives/bandages; cellulitis, open sores, or injury at or near the therapy site. Do not use while driving, operating machinery, or during any activity in which electrical stimulation could put the patient at risk of injury.
